🛍️ Retail Sales Dashboard – Power BI
📌 Project Overview

This Retail Sales Dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of sales performance across products, categories, regions, and time. Built using Power BI, the dashboard transforms raw retail data into meaningful business insights through interactive visuals and KPI tracking.

🎯 Business Objectives

Monitor total revenue and order count

Analyze year-over-year sales trends

Identify top-performing products

Evaluate category contribution to revenue

Compare regional sales performance

Track quantity sold by product

📊 Dashboard Components
🔢 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Total Revenue: 5M+

Total Orders: 117

Revenue summary card for quick executive view

These KPIs provide an instant snapshot of overall business health.

Line chart showing Sales LY vs Total Revenue

Drill-down hierarchy: Year → Quarter → Month → Day

Helps identify seasonality and growth patterns
